Put your wave / ocean videos in this folder.

How to upload:
  1. Open this "videos" folder on github.com.
  2. Click "Add file" -> "Upload files".
  3. Drag your video in, then click "Commit changes".

Tips:
  - Use short names with no spaces, e.g. waves.mp4 or sunrise-sea.mp4
  - The GitHub website accepts files up to 25 MB. For bigger files,
    download the 720p / smaller version from the stock site, or send
    the video to Claude in chat to compress it first.
  - MP4 works best.
